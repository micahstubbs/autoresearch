### Constraint

`TOTAL_BATCH_SIZE = 524288` and `MAX_SEQ_LEN = 2048` force `DEVICE_BATCH_SIZE` to divide 256 → valid choices are powers of 2 in `{1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128, 256}`. With BS=64 OOMing and BS=32 working, 32 is the optimal choice.

- RTX 4090, capability (8, 9), 24 GB VRAM (~3-4 GB used by other desktop processes)
- Driver 595.58.03, CUDA 13.2, torch 2.9.1+cu128
- MFU at BS=32: 10.0% (vs ~40% on H100; expected on consumer GPU)
